### Meeting records and issue-closing process

The minutes should be added to the meeting events e.g. [5 October 2026 event](https://www.w3.org/events/meetings/e1481531-144d-4051-aa5e-127fe26b9699/20261005T080000/).

The agreed process for closing issues is:

1. Mark issues as due for closing and discuss them at TF meetings.
2. Send them in a batch by email to the Working Group.
3. Close them if there are no objections after the following meeting.
